# Golf Coach visual catalog

System rules (level A, every picture): same golfer (navy polo, white trousers, white cap, brown belt, white shoes, white glove on the left hand), same studio light, white seamless background with real soft floor shadows, same render quality and sharpness, same scale within a sequence, annotations only when they teach, functional colours readable on white (green #287A4B correct, red #B93838 wrong, grey #66716A reference).

Representation types used (level B): **Single** position, **Pair** right and wrong with the same camera and crop, **Variants** two or three correct versions side by side, **Sequence** of the phases needed, **Ghost** a later pose over a faint earlier pose to show what moved, **Timeline** frames placed on a time axis, **Close up** of hands or clubface, **Overhead** view for alignment and rotation.

Views: F face on, D down the line, O overhead, C close up.
